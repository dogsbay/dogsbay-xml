## How to sign

DogsBay's contribution policy (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`) means most people will
never need to sign this. It is required only if You submit code — in a pull
request, or pasted into an issue or discussion.

**Individuals:** comment on your pull request or issue with:

```
I have read the DogsBay XML CLA and I hereby sign it on behalf of myself.
Name:    <your full legal name>
GitHub:  @<your username>
Email:   <your email>
Date:    <YYYY-MM-DD>
```

**On behalf of an employer or other legal entity**, that entity must agree
instead — the wording above binds only you personally, and clause 4 requires
authority you may not have. Contact us before submitting.

Signatures are recorded against the pull request or issue in which they are
given.
